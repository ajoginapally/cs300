Reflection Questions

1. Describe your experience/confidence in completing this project in 2 to 3 sentences.
   Even though exceptions at first was really confusing, the zybooks material really helped me understand it
   and apply it in the projec. For instance, handling and catching exceptions has become way easier
   and makes way more sense, and within this project I definitely think my confidence
   in these topics has deepened. 

2. How did exception handling improve your program's robustness?
   It made sure that there were no test-cases such that the program would break. It also ensured that no matter
   what user input was inputted, the program could handle all the edge cases in a way thats easy for a user to
   work with and understand.

3. This program uses custom exceptions. Did you think this was a good design choice? Why or why not?
   Yes, because instead of using Java developed ones, we had our own implementations that were oriented towards 
   the BankingSystem ideas. Also, normal exceptions most likely would not have worked for our use case, so 
   custom exceptions were definitely a good design choice. 

4. Did you try the test-driven development approach? If so, what did you think? If not, why did you choose not to?
   Once again, I had a normal approach: working from BankAccount to BankingSystem, and fianlly to the tester. 
   I think I chose to take a normal appraoch just because it seemed easier in my head to do, and is what I am
   accustomed to doing. In the future though, I will definitely consider doing a test-driven development.
